  - heading: "Upcoming event: Rohatsu Retreat 2026"
    text: |
      Rohatsu is the highlight of the Zen calendar year. It commemorates the Buddha’s enlightenment 2500 years ago, and each year in early December it is celebrated in Zen communities around the world.

      Our Rohatsu retreat will include sitting and walking meditation, liturgy, a Dharma talk, a qi gong or yoga movement session, opportunities to meet privately with a teacher, breakfast and lunch on Saturday, and a work practice period. On Saturday afternoon we will enjoy a movie and popcorn, followed by a community celebratory potluck dinner.

      ### Schedule

      - **Friday, Dec. 11, 6:30–9:00 PM:** Retreat begins
      - **Saturday, Dec. 12, 7:00 AM–3:00 PM:** Retreat continues
      - **Saturday, 3:00–5:00 PM:** Movie and popcorn
      - **Saturday, 5:00–7:00 PM:** Community potluck dinner and celebration

      ### How to register

      Please sign up before **5:00 PM on Friday, December 5**. Email us at [info@zenbridge.org](mailto:info@zenbridge.org), or go to the [Giving page](giving.html), click the Donate button, choose “Rohatsu Retreat” and make a payment.

      The suggested donation is $20–$50. A donation of any amount is gratefully accepted, and everyone is welcome regardless of ability to pay. If you have any questions about the retreat, [contact us](mailto:info@zenbridge.org).

  - heading: New to Zen meditation?
    style: highlight
    text: |
      We offer an Introduction to Meditation class on the first Saturday of the month, after the Saturday Dharma talk (a public talk on Zen Buddhism). The class runs from 9:30 to 10:30 AM.
