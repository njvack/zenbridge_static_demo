Upcoming Event: Rohatsu Retreat 2026
     
Rohatsu is the highlight of the Zen calendar year. It commemorates the Buddha’s enlightenment 2500 years ago and each year in early December it is celebrated in Zen communities around the world.  Our Rohatsu retreat will include sitting and walking meditation, liturgy, a Dharma talk, a qi gong or yoga movement session, opportunities to meet privately with a teacher, breakfast and lunch on Saturday, and a work practice period. On Saturday afternoon we will enjoy a movie and popcorn, followed by a community celebratory potluck dinner. 

See the schedule below
Retreat starts Friday, December 11, 6:30 - 9:00 PM
Retreat continues Saturday, December 12, 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Movie and popcorn Saturday, 3:00 -5:00 PM 
Community potluck dinner and celebration 5:00 - 7:00 PM

 Please sign up for this retreat before 5:00 PM Friday, December 5. 
 Suggested donation is $20.00 - $50.00. While a donation of any amount will be gratefully accepted, everyone is welcome regardless of ability to pay. If you have any questions or concerns about this retreat, please  contact us at info@zenbridge.org

​To register let us know by contacting us at info@zenbridge.org or go to the "giving" page, click the donate button, select "Rohatsu Retreat" and make a payment.

​New to Zen Meditation?
​
We offer an Introduction to Mediation class the first Saturday of the month after the Saturday dharma talk (public talk on Zen Buddhism). The class  is from 9:30 - 10:30 a.m. There is no need to register. You are welcome to join us for meditation before the class (no experience required) or just come at 9:30 for the class. It is free and open to all.
